## 摘要
AI智能体正日益部署于移动边缘环境中，通过多步工作流自主处理多样化任务。与传统具有预定义依赖结构的依赖感知任务不同，智能体工作流根据中间执行结果动态生成。为最小化作业完成时延的加权和，本文使用动态工作流图对部分揭示的智能体工作流建模，并构建一个在线调度问题，联合优化计算放置、无线资源块分配和边缘计算资源分配。为应对未来工作流演化的不确定性，我们首先将加权完成时间目标转化为等价的每时隙持有成本，并通过贝尔曼方程刻画最优策略。然后，基于已揭示的工作流和活动作业的估计剩余时延，构建一个可处理的前瞻性剩余成本代理。所提调度算法利用该代理确定计算放置，并利用由此得到的操作关键性在线分配无线与边缘计算资源。仿真结果表明，所提算法在不同场景下均优于基准方案。

## Abstract
AI agents are increasingly deployed in mobile edge environments to autonomously handle diverse tasks through multi-step workflows. Unlike conventional dependency-aware tasks with predefined dependency structures, agent workflows are dynamically generated based on intermediate execution results. To minimize the weighted sum of job completion latencies, this paper models partially revealed agent workflows using dynamic workflow graphs and formulates an online scheduling problem that jointly optimizes computation placement, wireless resource block allocation, and edge computing resource allocation. To address the uncertainty of future workflow evolution, we first transform the weighted completion time objective into an equivalent per-slot holding cost and characterize the optimal policy through a Bellman equation. We then construct a tractable look-ahead cost-to-go surrogate based on the revealed workflow and the estimated remaining latencies of active jobs. The proposed scheduling algorithm leverages the surrogate to determine computation placement and uses the resulting operation criticality to allocate wireless and edge computing resources online. Simulation results show that the proposed algorithm outperforms the benchmark schemes across diverse scenarios.
