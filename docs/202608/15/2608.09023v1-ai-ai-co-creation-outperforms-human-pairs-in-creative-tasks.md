## 摘要
以往研究常发现人工智能的创造力有限：单一系统很少能超越人类，而人机协作也未超过人类单独产出。我们认为这些结论低估了人工智能的潜力，因为大多数研究不允许循环、多智能体交换，而这些交换恰恰反映了支撑人类创造力的社会过程。我们进行了一项实验，比较了四种条件：(i) 具有互补生成器-评估器角色的AI-AI共同创作，(ii) 具有相同角色的AI-AI共同创作，(iii) 单一AI创作，以及(iv) 人类-人类共同创作。在三个开放式任务中，训练有素的评审员对1,212个想法在创造力、新颖性和实用性方面进行了评分。两种AI-AI共同创作条件在创造力和新颖性上始终优于单一AI创作和人类配对。实用性因任务而异：在最广泛且社会性最复杂的任务中，互补角色产生了最有用的解决方案，这表明当问题需要既富有想象力的构思又需要实际改进时，角色分化是有利的。人类配对表现最差，这与群体创造力中的产出损失一致。这些发现表明，结构化的、迭代的多智能体AI共同创作可以超越单一AI和人类-人类的构思。

## Abstract
Prior research often finds that AI creativity is limited: single systems rarely outperform humans, and human-AI collaboration does not exceed human output. We argue these conclusions underestimate AI's potential because most studies do not allow iterative, multi-agent exchanges that mirror the social processes underpinning human creativity. We conducted an experiment comparing four conditions: (i) AI-AI co-creation with complementary generator-evaluator roles, (ii) AI-AI co-creation with identical roles, (iii) single-AI creation, and (iv) human-human co-creation. Across three open-ended tasks, 1,212 ideas were rated by trained judges on creativity, novelty, and usefulness. Both AI-AI co-creation conditions consistently outperformed single-AI creation and human pairs on creativity and novelty. Usefulness varied by task: complementary roles yielded the most useful solutions in the broadest and most socially complex task, suggesting role differentiation is advantageous when problems require both imaginative ideation and practical refinement. Human pairs performed worst, consistent with production losses in group creativity. These findings indicate that structured, iterative multi-agent AI co-creation can exceed single-AI and human-human ideation.
